The mental calculation that referees perform during advantage decisions happens in approximately one-and-a-half to two seconds, analyzing attacking position relative to goal with central locations offering better advantage potential than wide areas, numerical situation comparing attackers versus defenders with even or favorable numbers supporting advantage while outnumbered attacks suggesting free kick preferable, possession security evaluating whether fouled player maintains control versus ball bouncing loose where advantage would gift possession to opponents, player skill assessment considering whether fouled team has technical ability to exploit advantage versus less skilled sides better served by set piece, and scoring probability judging whether current position offers realistic goal threat versus speculative chance that organized free kick might improve.
